DOES TEMPORAL PREDICTION ACTUALLY HELP? (GRU ABLATION STUDY)

Paired Ablation Analysis

Controlled paired ablation across 40 matched RF environments comparing V73 With GRU vs V73 Without GRU.

Scientifically Important Finding

"The scanned-cell Pd is essentially unchanged (78.61% with GRU vs 78.67% without GRU), indicating that the gain comes primarily from better scan allocation rather than improved receiver physics."

By forecasting where agile threats will transmit next, the scheduler allocates receiver time slots preemptively, increasing cumulative mission reward by +13.99 points (+9.47%), intercepting +6.90 more threat bursts (+4.36%), and improving band fairness by +3.60 percentage points without requiring wider RF front-end hardware.
